# Planetfall

Planetfall is a sci-fi roguelite with **Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor-style gameplay**: a top-down perspective in a 3D world, hordes of enemies, mining, loot, and extraction.

The player's persistent home base is an **upgradeable spaceship**, built and expanded with Minecraft-inspired construction and crafting. From its control room, players select planets, travel by FTL, and deploy to the surface in a drop pod.

On each planetary run, players fight waves of enemies—including Minecraft-inspired zombies and other creatures—gather resources, find loot, and attempt to extract safely. Successfully recovered materials and equipment return to the ship, enabling permanent upgrades, new construction, and further exploration.

**Core loop:** Build and prepare aboard the ship → choose a planet → FTL travel → drop pod deployment → survive, mine, and loot → extract → upgrade the ship → repeat.
